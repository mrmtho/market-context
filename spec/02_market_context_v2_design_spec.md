# Market Context v2 - Product Design Specification

## Vision

Most finance products answer:

> What is the price?

Market Context answers:

> Why was the price there?

The product is a visual investment research platform where price becomes the timeline through which investors explore company fundamentals, macroeconomic conditions, news, media, and market sentiment.

---

## Core Product Concept

### The Investment Time Machine

The user scrubs through a company's price history.

As they move through time, every other layer sitting behind the price layer auto updates:
- Price [front layer]
- EPS
- PE Ratio
- Revenue
- Margins
- Inflation
- Interest Rates
- GDP Growth
- Unemployment
- Commodity Prices
- News
- Earnings Reports
- YouTube Analysis
- Analyst Commentary

The user experiences:
'What did the world look like when the market priced this company here?'

## Design Principles

### Context First

Never show price alone.

### Time-Centric

Everything is linked to time.

### Explanation Over Data

Always explain why.

### Layered Discovery

Price → Fundamentals → Macro → Narrative → Discussion

### Research Flow

The product should feel like investigating a market moment, not reading a dashboard.

---

## Differentiation

Google Finance:
Price + News

Yahoo Finance:
Price + Metrics

Bloomberg:
Data Density

Market Context:
Price + Fundamentals + Macro + Narrative + Discussion synchronized through time.
